Welcome to MYP by: Nana Yaa Djan

Everyone was new here once or twice. Your facilitators and the students at Cornerstone are excited to have you join them.

You are not alone. This website will help you know what to expect before your first day.

A message from a student

"When I moved up from Grade 5, I was nervous about having lots of different teachers and finding my classes. Honestly, it's way less scary than it seems. Teachers are really helpful, and if you get lost, just ask any teacher. Write your timetable down and keep it in your bag, and don’t be scared to ask questions. You’ll make new friends faster than you think!"

A message from a Teacher

"Our classroom is a safe place. You can ask questions, make mistakes and try new things here, and nobody will laugh at you. Everyone in MYP 1 was new once, so we all remember how it feels. If you ever feel nervous or confused, come and talk to me or any other teacher. We are here to help you, and we are really looking forward to meeting you!"

Need help?

  • Feeling worried? Talk to your teachers or the school counselor. They are always happy to help.
  • Are you lost or confused? Ask any teacher or MYP student.
  • Is someone being rude or unkind to you? Tell a facilitator immediately. You will be listened to.
  • Have a question? Ask immediately.

Our promise

  • We will welcome you with a friendly smile
  • We will help you find your way
  • We will listen to you when you have a problem
  • We will ensure your safety

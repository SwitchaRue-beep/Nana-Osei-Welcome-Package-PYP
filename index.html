Mission

Our mission at Cornerstone International Academy is to empower all our students through the nurturing of their individual capabilities in the fast-changing world to be responsible and contribute to the world.

Vision

The vision of Cornerstone International Academy is to create a nationally known and internationally recognized school that produces action-oriented students and staff with loyalty, wisdom and responsibility for lifelong character.

IB Mission

The IB develops inquiring, knowledgeable and caring young people who help to create a better and more peaceful world through education that builds intercultural understanding and respect.
